# SYNCORA — AI Event Management Platform

SYNCORA is a real-time event intelligence platform that replaces scattered WhatsApp groups, tangled Excel sheets, and delayed vendor invoices with a single live source of truth.

## Brand & Aesthetic System
- **Strict Brand Palette**:
  - Pure Black (`#000000`), Near-Black Ink (`#07060d`), Panel (`#0d0b17`), Deep Navy (`#050b2e`)
  - Royal Blue (`#1d4ed8`), Azure (`#2f7be8`), Teal (`#0e9aa7`), Mint Highlight (`#d7f3e6`)
  - Lilac Glow (`#cdbbf0`), Mid Violet (`#6b4fb8`), Deep Indigo (`#3b2a8f`), Crimson Alert (`#8c2b3d`)
  - Accent: Chrome Gold (`#c9a77a`)
- **Assets**:
  - `/public/assets/logo.png` & `logo.svg`: High-contrast serif wordmark with wide-tracked subtitle.
  - `/public/assets/aurora.png` & `aurora.svg`: Iridescent diagonal beam with 3D glowing torus and fine film grain texture.
- **Typography**: Playfair Display (editorial luxury headings), Inter (UI controls), JetBrains Mono (tabular figures and KPIs).

## Routes & Workspaces
- `/` — Interactive Landing Page (Hero with 3D iridescent ring, Problem chaos collapse, 5-Phase Lifecycle, Four Perspectives, WhatsApp-to-Structured converter, AI Risk & Profit Optimizer, Connected Network Graph).
- `/login` — Role Chooser (4 animated tilt cards).
- `/login/owner` — Owner Login (Royal-to-violet accent, Company Access Code field, 1-click demo).
- `/login/event-head` — Event Head Login (Teal-to-azure accent, Active Event picker, 1-click demo).
- `/login/team-lead` — Team Lead Login (Violet-to-lilac accent, Department dropdown, 1-click demo).
- `/login/volunteer` — Volunteer Login (Mint-to-teal accent, multi-team picker [F&B, Production & AV, Registration, Stage Decor, Logistics, Hospitality], specific volunteer profile selection per team, 6-digit fast event code, and 1-click personalized sign-in).
- `/owner` — Company Command Center (Portfolio table/cards, KPIs, cross-event workload, financial ledger, AI assistant, 3-step event creation wizard).
- `/event-head` — Event Master Control (Live countdown, vendor procurement pipeline, dynamic budget what-if simulators, 9 department checklists, meeting hub with AI extraction, live mission control).
- `/team-lead` — Department Operations Deck (Progress rings, task board, volunteer roster, photo proof review queue, broadcast channel).
- `/volunteer` — Volunteer Ground View (390px mobile frame mockup, start/mark done, photo submission with GPS sign-off, attendance check-in, ground problem reporting).

## 4-Role Live Sync Walkthrough (Demo Script)
1. **Volunteer Ground Execution**:
   - Open Volunteer mode. Tap **"Start Task Now"** on *"Buffet Setup & Tasting Verification"*.
   - Tap **"Attach Photo & Mark Done"** and submit the proof photo.
   - Confetti explodes in brand colors, and a live toast notification fires.
2. **Team Lead Verification**:
   - Switch to **Team Lead** using the persistent top-right Role Switcher pill.
   - Notice the Department progress ring immediately increments.
   - Check the **Review Queue** tab; tap **"Inspect Proof"** or **"Approve"**.
3. **Event Head Live Feed**:
   - Switch to **Event Head** view.
   - Notice the Planning Progress percentage automatically recalculated.
   - The right rail **Permanent Activity Ledger** has appended the verified record with timestamp and photo lightbox.
4. **Owner Company Command Center**:
   - Switch to **Company Owner** view.
   - Observe the portfolio health score and company profit metrics updating in real-time.
